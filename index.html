A few good questions.

What is Spinbuild?

This page is a workflow concept: a simple illustration of moving from an idea to a plan and a next step. The playground lets you explore three sample paths.

Do I need an account?

No. The interactive demo runs in your browser without registration or a password.

Does the demo save my work?

No. Sample selections and progress only last while this page is open. No projects are created and no external services are triggered.
